Function: eca-chat-inline-toggle-overlays

eca-chat-inline-toggle-overlays is an interactive and natively compiled function defined in eca-chat-inline.el.

Signature

(eca-chat-inline-toggle-overlays)

Documentation

Toggle visibility of all inline overlays.

Hidden overlays keep streaming in the background and their anchors get their keys back; they are shown again on the next toggle or inline prompt.

Key Bindings

This command is not in any keymaps.

(defun eca-chat-inline-toggle-overlays ()
  "Toggle visibility of all inline overlays.
Hidden overlays keep streaming in the background and their
anchors get their keys back; they are shown again on the next
toggle or inline prompt."
  (interactive)
  (eca-chat-inline--set-overlays-hidden
   (not eca-chat-inline--overlays-hidden))
  (eca-info "Inline overlays %s"
            (if eca-chat-inline--overlays-hidden "hidden" "shown")))
