Function: pdf-util-scale-to

pdf-util-scale-to is a natively compiled function defined in pdf-util.el.

Signature

(pdf-util-scale-to LIST-OF-EDGES FROM TO &optional ROUNDING-FN)

Documentation

Scale LIST-OF-EDGES in FROM basis to TO.

FROM and TO should both be a cons (WIDTH . HEIGHT). See also pdf-util-scale.

(defun pdf-util-scale-to (list-of-edges from to &optional rounding-fn)
  "Scale LIST-OF-EDGES in FROM basis to TO.

FROM and TO should both be a cons \(WIDTH . HEIGHT\).  See also
`pdf-util-scale'."

  (pdf-util-scale list-of-edges
                  (cons (/ (float (car to))
                           (float (car from)))
                        (/ (float (cdr to))
                           (float (cdr from))))
                  rounding-fn))
