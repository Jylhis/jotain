Function: logview-reset-manual-entry-hiding

logview-reset-manual-entry-hiding is an interactive and natively compiled function defined in logview.el.

Signature

(logview-reset-manual-entry-hiding)

Documentation

Show all manually hidden entries in the buffer.

Entries currently not visible due to filtering are also cleared of “manually hidden” mark. However, such entries will remain invisible until filters are removed or changed appropriately.

Key Bindings

logview-mode-map r h

(defun logview-reset-manual-entry-hiding ()
  "Show all manually hidden entries in the buffer.

Entries currently not visible due to filtering are also cleared
of “manually hidden” mark.  However, such entries will remain
invisible until filters are removed or changed appropriately."
  (interactive)
  (logview--assert)
  (logview--retire-hiding-symbol 'logview--hidden-entry-symbol)
  (logview--update-invisibility-spec))
