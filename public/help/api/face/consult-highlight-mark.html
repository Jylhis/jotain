Face: consult-highlight-mark

Face Sample:

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

Documentation:

Face used for mark positions in completion candidates. Used for example by `consult-mark'. The face should be different than the `cursor' face to avoid confusion.

Defined in `consult.el'.
